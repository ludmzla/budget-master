Import bank statement

Works with Volksbank / VR-Banking exports (and most other banks' CSV files). Your file is read right here in the browser and never uploaded.
  1. Log in to online banking at voba-oberberg.de (or the VR Banking app on desktop).
  2. Open your account and go to Umsätze (transactions), then pick a date range.
  3. Choose Exportieren → CSV and save the file.
  4. Drop it below. Duplicates are skipped, so overlapping date ranges are fine.
